Modules

Five modules, covered in order from basics to deployment.

Module 1Introduction to Data Science

Foundations of data science — what it is, the skills and tools it demand, and the four families of analysis that decide what you actually do with the data once you have it.

 Module 2Data Extraction

This module introduces the process of obtaining raw data from diverse sources, including databases, APIs, web crawling, and flat files. It covers the tools and techniques used to extract and load data into a usable format.

 Module 3Data Visualization

The matplotlib API from first principles, then the pandas and seaborn plotting surface on top of it — every core plot type the lab record actually uses.

 Module 4Statistical Thinking

The longest module, and the statistical backbone of the course — distributions and how to summarise them, PMFs and CDFs, percentile ranks, and the three models that cover most real measurements.

 Module 5Time Series Analysis and Predictive Modeling

Data with a time index behaves differently. Cleaning and plotting a series, smoothing it, measuring its self-similarity, then building and evaluating models against it.

Experiments

Six interactive experiments. Click one to compile, execute, and inspect live output.

Exp 1Hierarchical Indexing: Series

Create and manipulate MultiIndex Series with department and branch levels, slicing and label indexing.

 Exp 2Hierarchical Indexing: DataFrame

Build MultiIndex DataFrames from tuples and perform reshape operations with stack() and unstack().

 Exp 3Data Cleaning & Preprocessing

Detect missing data with isna(), dropna() & fillna(), map categories, detect outliers, and split text with regex.

 Exp 4Indexing, Reshaping & Merging

Series MultiIndexing, tabular stack/unstack, and merging datasets using outer join and combine_first().

 Exp 5Data Visualization with Matplotlib & Seaborn

Explore the Iris dataset with statistical summaries, line plots, scatter plots, histograms, and subplots.

Exp 6Time Series Analysis

Build time series from dates, localize and convert time zones, work with Periods and frequencies, and resample data.
